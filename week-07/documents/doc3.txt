Artificial Intelligence Adoption in Pakistan's Public Sector

Government bodies in Pakistan, including provincial IT boards, have begun exploring generative AI tools to improve internal efficiency and citizen-facing services. Common early use cases include automated drafting of routine correspondence, summarization of lengthy policy documents for quick executive review, and chatbot assistants that answer frequently asked questions about government procedures. Internship and training programs have introduced young professionals to prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation, and embedding-based semantic search as foundational skills for building AI-powered internal tools. A recurring theme in these pilot projects is the importance of data privacy when processing citizen information through AI systems, since public-sector data often includes sensitive personal details. Agencies are also experimenting with AI-assisted meeting note generation and document question-answering systems to reduce the administrative burden on staff who currently spend significant time manually searching through PDFs and reports. Early results suggest that semantic search, which retrieves information based on meaning rather than exact keyword matches, significantly outperforms traditional keyword search when users phrase queries in natural language rather than using the exact terminology found in source documents.
